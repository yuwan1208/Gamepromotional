01 / OVERVIEW

Game Overview

The Forgotten Village is a dark fantasy adventure centered on a mysterious undead invasion, the disappearance of villagers, and a rescue mission that reveals a greater danger beneath the mountains.

Game Title

The Forgotten Village

Genre / Setting

Adventure RPG, Dark Fantasy, and Action-Adventure. The game features exploration, combat encounters, quest progression, undead enemies, and mysterious locations within a dark fantasy world.

Main Objective

Find the missing villager, uncover the truth behind the undead invasion, and confront the threat connected to the Giant Golem.

Central Conflict

Villagers are disappearing while skeletons and zombies appear around the abandoned settlement.

02 / BACKGROUND

Background Story

Several years ago, the Forgotten Village was one of the kingdom's most peaceful settlements. Its people lived through farming, trading, and hunting.

Everything changed after a mysterious earthquake shook the mountains nearby. Strange events followed: voices came from the forest, animals disappeared without tracks, bodies vanished from the cemetery, skeletons and zombies appeared at night, and villagers began disappearing.

The village guards could not stop the growing number of undead creatures. Most residents fled to nearby settlements, while only a few remained.

One night, a young villager named Thomas approached the player for help. His older sister had disappeared after entering the abandoned village while searching for their father. Thomas believes she may still be alive because he sometimes hears her voice coming from the village.

The player's journey begins with a request for help — and leads toward a secret buried beneath the mountain.
03 / CHARACTERS

Main Characters

The Player

The character controlled by the player. They accept Thomas's request, investigate the abandoned areas, face the undead, and help rescue Anna.

Thomas

A young villager who asks the player to find his missing older sister. She went looking for their father and did not return.

Anna

Thomas's older sister. She is found among the villagers held underground and warns the player about the Ancient Guardian.

The Necromancer

The antagonist in the underground crypt. He has been kidnapping villagers and using dark magic to create an undead army.

The Giant Golem

Also called the Ancient Guardian. It is the greater threat beneath the mountain. The story reveals that it is awakening, but does not show it being defeated.

04 / STORY

Story Progression

The Missing Sister

At the village entrance, Thomas explains that Anna went into the Forgotten Village three days earlier while searching for their father. She had mentioned strange lights coming from the old forest. Thomas asks the player to find her, beginning the objective to investigate the Forgotten Forest.

The Search Through the Forgotten Areas

The player travels through the forest and abandoned village, encounters undead creatures, and finds clues about what happened to the settlement. These clues lead toward an underground crypt.

The Necromancer's Secret

Inside the crypt, the player discovers that the Necromancer has been kidnapping villagers and using dark magic to create an undead army. The player confronts him and finds Anna and other trapped villagers.

The Truth Beneath the Mountain

Anna explains that the Necromancer was gathering people to awaken something buried beneath the mountain: the Ancient Guardian. The ground begins shaking, the dungeon walls crack, and the player helps the villagers escape through an underground passage.

A Warning of the Giant Golem

Back at the village, Thomas and Anna reunite. Anna explains that the Necromancer feared the Giant Golem and could not control its power. A distant roar echoes from the mountains, and a gigantic shadow moves behind them. The story ends with the greater threat still looming.

05 / CHAPTERS

Chapters & Quests

Quest Introduction
The Missing Sister

Location: Village Entrance

Meet Thomas and accept his request to find Anna.

Objective: Investigate the Forgotten Forest.
Chapter One
The Forest of the Dead

The player enters a silent forest, encounters skeleton warriors, and discovers an abandoned campsite and an old journal inside a watchtower.

Objective: Defeat 40 Skeleton Warriors.
Chapter Two
The Village That Never Sleeps

The player explores the damaged village, sees strange symbols on walls, and encounters zombies inside abandoned houses.

Objective: Defeat 20 Zombies.
Chapter Three
The Necromancer's Secret

The player discovers the Necromancer's role in the disappearances and the creation of the undead army.

Objective: Defeat the Necromancer and rescue Thomas's sister.
Chapter Four
The Truth Beneath the Mountain

Anna reveals the Necromancer's plan involving the Ancient Guardian. A disturbance begins as the buried threat awakens.

Objective: Help the rescued villagers escape through the underground passage.
Quest Ending
A Warning of the Giant Golem

Thomas and Anna reunite. The player learns that the Giant Golem is beyond the Necromancer's control.

Story outcome: The villagers return while the Giant Golem remains a looming threat.
06 / WORLD

Game World & Locations

Village Entrance

The starting location where Thomas approaches the player and explains that Anna is missing.

Forgotten Forest

A quiet forest where the player encounters skeleton warriors and discovers an abandoned watchtower and campsite.

Forgotten Village

An abandoned settlement with damaged gates, empty houses, wandering zombies, and strange symbols.

Underground Crypt

A dungeon containing undead creatures, magical symbols, the Necromancer's chamber, and trapped villagers.

Mountain Beneath the Village

The place associated with the Ancient Guardian, whose awakening is revealed near the end of the story.

07 / THREATS

Enemies & Antagonists

Skeleton Warriors

Undead enemies encountered in the Forgotten Forest. Chapter One sets an objective to defeat 40.

Zombies

Undead creatures found wandering through the abandoned village. Chapter Two sets an objective to defeat 20.

The Necromancer

A magical antagonist who fires projectiles toward the player and can restore some health if the player takes too long to defeat him.

The Giant Golem / Ancient Guardian

A powerful being buried beneath the mountain. Its awakening is foreshadowed at the end of the current story.

08 / OBJECTIVES

Main Objectives

  1. Meet Thomas at the village entrance and accept his request.
  2. Investigate the Forgotten Forest.
  3. Defeat the skeleton warriors and search the abandoned watchtower.
  4. Explore the Forgotten Village and defeat the zombies.
  5. Enter the underground crypt and uncover the Necromancer's secret.
  6. Defeat the Necromancer and rescue Anna and the other trapped villagers.
  7. Help the rescued villagers escape and return to the village.
  8. Learn the truth about the Giant Golem and the danger beneath the mountain.
