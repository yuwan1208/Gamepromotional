ABOUT THE GAME

A JOURNEY THROUGH A WORLD OF MYSTERY

The Forgotten Village is an action RPG experience set in a fantastical world shaped by mystery, conflict, and the power of light. Explore striking environments, face powerful adversaries, and uncover the secrets that lie beyond what the eye can see.

As the journey unfolds, players are invited to discover the world and piece together the truth behind its strange and beautiful illusions.

THE LORE

BEYOND THE FRACTURED LIGHT

Beyond the quiet hills lies a peaceful village, where life has always been simple and undisturbed. But beneath its calm and familiar beauty, a forgotten darkness begins to stir. When a lone swordsman's beloved sister mysteriously vanishes into the forest, he sets out to find her, armed with nothing but his blade and the hope of bringing her home.

Yet the deeper he ventures into the woods, the more the world around him begins to change. Strange illusions, forgotten ruins, and terrifying creatures reveal that the forest holds a secret far darker than anyone could have imagined.
